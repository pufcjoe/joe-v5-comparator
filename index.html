Methodology and limitations

Score = 0.40 target similarity + 0.25 treble smoothness + 0.20 overall curve smoothness + 0.15 tonal balance. Log-spaced comparisons, median baseline removal, prominence-aware local residual penalties and frequency-dependent reliability. Scores are heuristic, not psychoacoustically calibrated. High-treble data above 8 kHz has reduced confidence. Do not mix measurement rigs or compensated/uncompensated datasets. IEM 711 targets are not automatically valid for over-ear headphones.
